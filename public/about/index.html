About

Shedding light on the mysterious side of tattooing

The Science of Tattooing was established by Hannah Wolf to bring awareness and understanding to under-researched areas of tattooing.

At a glance

Hannah Wolf is a veteran tattoo artist with more than twenty years behind the machine. In 2017 she began working with PhDs and MDs on a textbook about tattoo materials, and in 2018 founded The Science of Tattooing to share that knowledge.

The project's goals are to share existing research, encourage further study and support better procedure standards — especially for clients with medical conditions.

What we make

  • The Science of Tattooing, the first book in a planned series for operators, lawmakers and consumers.
  • A gamified learning app with 183 lessons across six subjects.
  • Education for regulators and enforcement agencies.
