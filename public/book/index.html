Why this book exists

In 2017, tattoo artist Hannah Wolf began working with PhDs and MDs to write a textbook on tattoo materials. Tattooing is one of the oldest art forms, yet much of its science has gone under-researched or unshared. This book gathers existing research into one place, encourages further study and supports better procedure standards — particularly for clients with medical conditions.

It is the first in a planned series for operators, lawmakers and consumers.
