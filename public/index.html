Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is in tattoo ink?

Tattoo ink is a suspension of pigments (the particles that create color) in a carrier liquid, often with additives such as binders, thickeners and preservatives. Pigments may be organic or inorganic, and formulations vary widely by brand. The book and app explain each component and why it matters.

Do I need to download the app?

No. The Science of Tattooing app runs in your web browser. Open it on any phone or computer and start a lesson right away — no download required.

Who is The Science of Tattooing for?

Tattoo artists and apprentices who want a scientific foundation for their craft, clients and collectors who want to understand what goes into their skin, and regulators and lawmakers who write and enforce tattoo safety rules.

Is this medical advice?

No. The Science of Tattooing shares published research and fundamentals for education. For a specific medical condition, talk with a physician before getting tattooed.

How does laser tattoo removal work?

Laser removal delivers very short pulses of light that shatter ink particles into smaller fragments, which the body's immune system then gradually clears. The book covers the physics and physiology behind it.
